Seymour's tight heptagram

The Paley tournament on Z_7: vertex v beats v+1, v+2 and v+4, the nonzero squares mod 7 (arcs in rose, violet, blue by step, brightening towards their heads), so every pair of vertices is joined once, in one direction. Seymour's second-neighbourhood conjecture says every oriented graph has a vertex with at least as many vertices two steps away as one step away. Here every vertex is exactly tight: its first ring v + {1, 2, 4} has 3 vertices, its second ring v + {3, 5, 6} (reached in two steps, not one) has 3, and together with v they are all 7.
