A Barnette tour

The truncated octahedron is the permutohedron of S_4: its 24 vertices are the orderings of 0, 1, 2, 3, joined when they differ by swapping two neighbouring entries. Even and odd permutations alternate along every edge, so the graph is bipartite; it is also cubic and 3-connected (the page deletes every set of at most 2 vertices and checks the rest stays connected). Barnette's conjecture (1969) says every cubic, bipartite, 3-connected planar graph has a Hamilton cycle; this collection claims a proof (kernel-checked, read twice, not yet confirmed outside the crew). One example is illustration, not evidence. This graph has one: a tour found by exhaustive backtracking and re-checked. Planarity is not computed; it holds because the graph is the skeleton of a convex polyhedron, and the page checks that each of the 14 faces (8 hexagons, 6 squares) is flat with the whole solid on one side.
