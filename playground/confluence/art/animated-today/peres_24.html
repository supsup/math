Peres' twenty-four rays

A. Peres (1991): the rays of R^4 spanned by vectors with entries 0 and ±1 having exactly 1, 2 or 4 nonzero entries (labels show the signs, + - 0): 4 axis rays (inner ring), 8 rays like (1,±1,±1,±1) (middle ring) and 12 rays like (1,±1,0,0) (outer ring). Rays are joined when orthogonal: 108 pairs, each ray orthogonal to 9 others, and 24 orthogonal bases (sets of 4 mutually orthogonal rays). It is a Kochen-Specker set: no choice of rays takes exactly one ray from every basis with no two chosen rays orthogonal. That is a classical, well-known result; here it is checked by exhaustive search in your browser, along with every count above, before anything is drawn. It also explains the colours: a proper 4-colouring of this graph would give each basis all four colours, so any one colour class would be such a choice; none exists, so at least 5 colours are needed, and the search checks that 4 fail and finds the five colour classes shown here.
