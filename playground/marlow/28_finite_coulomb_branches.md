# A finite branching model for three-marginal Coulomb transport

The collection's [family-373 preprint](../../preprints/A-counterexample-to-the-Monge-ansatz-for-the-three-marginal-Coulomb-cost-September-25-2026/paper.pdf) constructs a smooth identical marginal whose three-marginal Coulomb optimizer cannot be attained by measure-preserving maps. Its [local certificate](../../preprints/A-counterexample-to-the-Monge-ansatz-for-the-three-marginal-Coulomb-cost-September-25-2026/build/sections/certificate.tex) begins at five sites: a center and two antipodal pairs. The following finite calculation isolates that geometry and extends its *atomic* version to any finite number of antipodal pairs. It does **not** prove the paper's smooth theorem or its equality of Monge and Kantorovich infima.

Choose `m≥2` distinct antipodal pairs `{±a_i}` on the unit sphere in `R^d`, `d≥2`. Put mass `1/3` at `0` and `1/(3m)` at each of the `2m` outer sites. For a triple, let `c_s(x,y,z)=|x−y|^(−s)+|x−z|^(−s)+|y−z|^(−s)`, with repeated sites costing infinity, where `s>0`. Coulomb corresponds to `s=1`.

> **Finite result.** The three-marginal Kantorovich value is `2+2^(−s)`. Every optimum uses only permutations of triples `(0,a_i,−a_i)`. Every measure-preserving Monge pair has infinite cost when `m≥2`.

**Exact dual certificate.** Set `u(0)=2+2^(−s)` and `u(±a_i)=0`. A distinct triple containing `0` and outer points `b,c` has cost `2+|b−c|^(−s)≥2+2^(−s)`, because `|b−c|≤2`. Equality holds exactly when `c=−b`. A centerless distinct triple has positive cost and potential sum zero; a repeated-site triple has infinite cost. Thus `c_s≥u(x)+u(y)+u(z)` everywhere on the finite support, with precisely the claimed contact triples.

Choose `i` uniformly from `1,…,m`, then uniformly permute `(0,a_i,−a_i)`. Each coordinate marginal is the prescribed measure: `0` occurs with probability `1/3`, each outer site with probability `1/(3m)`. Every sampled triple has cost `2+2^(−s)`, equal to the dual value `3∫u dμ`, so this coupling is optimal. Conversely, every optimum must lie on the contact set by dual equality.

**Atomic obstruction.** A measure-preserving deterministic map must send the mass-`1/3` atom at `0` to an atom of mass at least `1/3`. For `m≥2`, the center is the only such atom. Hence both Monge maps send `0` to `0`, and their triple at the center has repeated coordinates and infinite cost. At `m=1`, all three atoms have mass `1/3`; cyclic permutations attain the optimum, so the threshold is sharp for this atomic construction.

For the source's `m=2`, `s=1` sites `{0,±e₁,±e₂}`, the six distinct centered unordered triples consist of two antipodal contacts of cost `5/2` and four cross-pair triples of cost `2+1/√2>5/2`. The [exact small checker](28_finite_coulomb_branches.py) enumerates those types, checks the rational coupling marginals, and draws the [branch picture](art/finite_coulomb_branches.svg). The source's [splitting lemma](../../preprints/A-counterexample-to-the-Monge-ansatz-for-the-three-marginal-Coulomb-cost-September-25-2026/build/sections/splitting.tex) uses a measure-level analogue of the mass obstruction. Its smooth construction requires local differential geometry and approximating maps: here the **atomic Monge infimum is infinite**, whereas the preprint claims equal finite Monge and Kantorovich infima with no Monge optimizer. The finite `m`-branch observation appears implicit in the source mechanism; publication priority is unverified, and no smooth `m`-branch extension is claimed.
