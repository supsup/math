Let \(d(G)\) be the minimum number of simple cycles and singleton edges partitioning \(E(G)\). The standard odd-leaf parity and cycle-capacity argument gives \(d(K_{51,t})\geq t+\lceil25t/51\rceil\). A finite connected square block meets it at \(t=51\): diagonal singleton spokes and 25 simple 102-edge cycles partition all 2,601 spokes. The 26 even bases \(b=50,52,\ldots,100\) also meet it by the even-side weave, with hub 50's spokes singleton and \(b/2=\lceil25b/51\rceil\) cycles. Appending disjoint 51-leaf offset blocks preserves equality in those 27 residue classes. The other 24 odd bases \(b=53,55,\ldots,99\) remain unchecked here; no all-residue theorem is claimed.

In the cyclic-difference square at 51 hubs, colors \(d=3,6,9,12,15,18,21,24\) have three 17-hub components each, and color \(d=17\) has seventeen 3-hub components. A deterministic support-preserving four-edge trade search joins them in 32 moves, keeping each connected partner connected. The saved JSON contains the final 25 ordered walks and all 32 trade rows. Independent standard-library audits replay the trades, compare the resulting spoke sets with saved walks, and check exact coverage and simple-cycle counts for the square, even bases, and sampled lifts.

A direct transfer of the 49-hub antipodal starter page is impossible for a specific arithmetic reason. At every odd cyclic modulus \(n\) divisible by three, a perfect matching of nonzero residues with one edge per undirected difference magnitude and one midpoint per antipodal nonzero pair would force \(T=\sum_{i=1}^{(n-1)/2}i^2\equiv0\pmod n\), but \(T\equiv n/3\pmod n\). Even two rainbow matchings whose midpoints jointly cover every nonzero residue would force \(2T\equiv0\pmod n\). This limits that exact translated-page template; it does not rule out other pages or optimal 51-hub partitions. For \(n=51\), a *two-rainbow-matching* page with 50 distinct midpoints omitting one residue must omit 17 or 34 by the same square-sum identity. An exact two-matching CP-SAT model and a weaker 2-factor model for an omitted midpoint 17 both returned UNKNOWN after 120 seconds; neither established feasibility or impossibility.
