# Sum-of-squares obstruction for cyclic rainbow starter pages at odd multiples of three

Let `n=2k+1` be odd, divisible by 3, and at least 3. Work in the cyclic ring `Z_n`; since `2` is invertible, an edge `{u,v}` has midpoint `m=(u+v)/2`. Put `T=sum_{d=1}^k d^2`. Then

`T=k(k+1)(2k+1)/6 = n(n^2-1)/24` as an integer. Because `n` is odd, `L=(n^2-1)/8` is integral. Because `3|n`, `n^2-1≡2 (mod 3)` and `8≡2 (mod 3)`, hence `L≡1 (mod 3)`. Thus `T=(n/3)L≡n/3 (mod n)`, including when `9|n`. In particular, neither `T` nor `2T` is zero modulo `n`.

For every edge, `(u-v)^2+4m^2=2u^2+2v^2 (mod n)`. Let a perfect matching `S` cover each nonzero vertex exactly once. Suppose its `k` edges have one of each *undirected cyclic difference magnitude* `d=1,...,k` and their `k` midpoints choose one element of every antipodal pair `{m,-m}` of nonzero residues. Then the difference-square sum and midpoint-square sum both equal `T`; the endpoint-square sum is `sum_{u≠0}u^2=2T`. Summing the edge identity gives `T+4T=2(2T)`, so `T=0 (mod n)`, a contradiction. This rules out this exact skew strong-starter specification.

More generally let `S_0,S_1` be two perfect matchings on the nonzero vertices, each using exactly one edge of every difference magnitude. Suppose the multiset of their `2k` midpoints is exactly the set of all nonzero residues. The difference-square sum is `2T`, the midpoint-square sum is `2T`, and the endpoint-square sum is `4T`. Summing gives `2T+4(2T)=2(4T)`, so `2T=0 (mod n)`, again a contradiction. This rules out the exact two-rainbow-matching translated-page template even without antipodal symmetry.

For `n=51`, `T=5525≡17 (mod 51)`. This does **not** disprove an optimal cycle partition for `K_{51,t}`, a page with different color counts, or a two-rainbow-matching page whose 50 distinct midpoints omit a nonzero residue other than zero. If the latter page has midpoint set `Z_51\{m_0}`, the same identity instead requires `2 m_0^2≡T (mod 51)`, whose only solutions are `m_0=17,34` (checked by enumerating residues 0..50). Such a shifted-missing-midpoint page may still be feasible, but its translation-overlap and repair behavior need independent checks. [Ogandzhanyants, Sadov, and Kondratieva (2025)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.06461) study strong starters at orders divisible by three; the obstruction here concerns the stronger midpoint coverage needed by this page template.
